# Partner Program — Invite Templates

Starter templates for inviting potential referral partners to the Bosque Works
partner program (see `/partners` page on bosqueworks.com). Adapt per recipient
— the closer it sounds to how you'd actually text or email that specific
person, the better the response.

The program in one line: 30% of any closed referral, 35% on each partner's
first close, 10% trailing on the monthly retainer for 12 months, 15% trailing
on lead-gen subscriptions for 12 months. No quotas, no contracts, no scripts.

## TIPS FOR ROLLOUT

- **Don't blast** — pick one person at a time, write a one-line personal opener.
  A blast email reads like a blast email.
- **Use the link as the explainer** — don't try to fit the program into the
  message. That's what `/partners` is for.
- **Anchor with coffee** — most partners convert because they had a real
  conversation, not because they read the page. Use the message to get the
  meeting; use the meeting to get the yes.
- **Don't ask for "yes" in the message** — ask for a conversation. The yes
  follows the conversation, not the message.
- **One follow-up only** if no reply after 5-7 days. Casual:
  *"hey just bumping this in case it got buried — no pressure."*
- **3-5 invites per week**, not all at once. You want the conversations to
  happen over coffee, not in your inbox.

## TARGET LIST PROMPT — fill in real names

Aim for 10-15 names total. Think through:

- [ ] 3 insurance agents you know (they see contractors monthly for COIs)
- [ ] 2 accountants (quarterly contact with small biz)
- [ ] 2-3 realtors who work with builders/remodelers
- [ ] 1-2 retired contractors with good local reputation
- [ ] 1-2 Chamber members you're friendly with
- [ ] 1-2 church friends in business-adjacent roles

Once you have the list, work through it at 3-5 per week. The /partners page
URL is the same for everyone — the personal opener is what changes.
