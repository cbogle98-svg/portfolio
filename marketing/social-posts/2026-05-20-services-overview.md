to run one?") that opened with engagement-bait. Cody picked the statement
hook because it reads more authoritative — names the three needs as facts,
then names BW as the answer, then leaves the door open. Less salesy than
the question form.

## Production notes (for next post)

- **Skip hashtags on Facebook.** They look spammy in this context, unlike
  Instagram. Use the location-tag field instead for local discoverability.
- **Pair with an image.** Single image beats no image on FB by a wide
  margin. A side-by-side screenshot of one of the live demos (Hamilton
  Trailer Repair or BC Construction read well visually) or a composite of
  3-4 demos in a grid work best.
- **Posting window:** 9-11am or 5-7pm CT on weekdays for B2B-leaning
  small-biz audiences in TX. Avoid weekends if the goal is reaching other
  business owners.
- **Above-the-fold length:** this post shows fully without a "see more"
  truncation on mobile. Keep future posts under ~80 words for the same
  reason.
